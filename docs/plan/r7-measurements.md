# Windows 10 Mobile fidelity measurements, round 3 (R7)

Extends R3 (`w10m-measurements.md`) and R6 (`r6-measurements.md`) to four new targets: the Phone app (§1), the Messaging app (§2), Cortana's Notebook (§3), and Action center and volume panel motion (§4). Nothing below is estimated: every value is measured from a cited frame or quoted from a cited Microsoft document, or the row says UNMEASURED with what was tried.

Governing build (owner ruling): the final W10M release (15063 Creators Update / 15254 Fall Creators Update). 14393 footage is used where the element did not visibly change; earlier builds are flagged per row.

Evidence root (scratchpad, shared with other agents): `$R7 = /tmp/claude-1000/-home-jeremyking/283f1050-f733-41ac-a752-f9b0871bdcbe/scratchpad/r7-work/`. Frames are under `$R7/frames/`, contact sheets under `$R7/sheets/`, scripts under `$R7/scripts/`, per-frame data under `$R7/data/`. `$R3` and `$R6` are the earlier evidence roots (`…/scratchpad/r3-work/`, `…/scratchpad/r6-work/`). Headline answers (details and evidence in the rows cited):
- **Phone (§1):** there are three tabs, History / Speed dial / Dial pad. There is no Contacts tab and no Voicemail tab: "Phone book" and "Voicemail" are app-bar buttons (1.1.1, 1.1.8, 1.7.1). Tabs are icons on a W/5 pitch (1.1.2). The tab switch is a one-frame cut with the underline jumping, not a pivot slide (1.8.2). Dial pad: 3 × 4 flat borderless keys on W/3 columns, letters to the right of the digit. Rows stretch to the Call bar: pitch 50 epx on a 320x533 canvas, 64 epx on 360x640 (1.2.1–1.2.3). A pressed key fills its whole cell with accent for one frame (1.2.4). History rows: 48-epx circular avatar, 70.5-epx pitch for two-line rows and 88 epx with a SIM line, no separators (1.3). Incoming call (final-build camera only): circular photo, name, number, and a full-width accent "Slide up" bar that hops 30 epx every 2.48 s (1.5.1, 1.5.3). In-call buttons: Hold / Add call / Bluetooth, then Video / Mute / Speaker / Dial pad, then an End call bar. After a dropped call the page lingers ≈5 s with greyed buttons (1.6.1, 1.6.5). There is no screen recording of incoming or in-call on 14393+, so those rows are MEDIUM/LOW.
- **Messaging (§2):** conversation list rows are 81.7 epx with a 48-epx avatar at 12 epx, the same in epx at 350 % and 400 % (2.1). There is a swipe-to-delete red panel and a long-press Mute/Delete menu (2.2). Compose row: paperclip at 19 epx, "Type a message", send arrow, 47 epx tall. The To box gets a white fill with a 2-epx accent border when focused (2.3.3–2.3.4). Attach menu: Photo or video, Camera, Your location, Contact, Voice note, Ringtone (2.3). List → New message is a 2-black-frame cut, then a slide up of ≈24 epx with a fade-in, 90 % by ≈257 ms (2.6.1). **No 14393+ footage of an open thread was found.** Bubbles (square accent rectangles with small corner tails) come from 2015/14376 footage only (LOW, 2.4). The governing-build thread, group thread, received picture and send action are UNMEASURED (2.4.6, 2.5.1, 2.5.2, 2.3.10).
- **Notebook (§3):** the ≡ pane is 256 epx wide with "CORTANA" / Home / Notebook / Reminders at the top and Settings / Feedback anchored above the query bar. Rows are on a 48-epx pitch, laid out in epx (3.1). The Notebook is a flat list of 44-epx rows (20-epx outline icon + one label), with a settings group (About me, Quiet Hours, Connected Services, Permissions) above the categories. It is **a light page even in dark theme on 15063** and loads its content after the page appears (3.3). Page titles sit at 60 epx left, 14-epx cap (3.3.2). Opening a page shows ≈283–300 ms of black, then a 200–317 ms ease-out fade-in (3.2). Reminders is its own ≡ destination: on 14393 it is grouped Today / Coming up / Whenever with 60-epx rows (3.5). The 15063/15254 Reminders page is UNMEASURED (3.9.1).
- **Action center / volume (§4):** the panel **tracks the finger 1:1** and holds partial positions. On release it runs a fixed ≈290 ms animation whatever the distance left, and an upward flick closes it from any height (4.1.2, 4.1.4, 4.2.2). The background dim is tied to how far the panel is open (4.1.8). Quick-action expand ≈200 ms and collapse ≈283 ms, with bezier fits and residuals given (4.3.2–4.3.3). Notifications swipe right, track the finger and dim to ≈0.4. Released at 73 % they dismissed; at ≈30 % they snapped back. The threshold is UNMEASURED (4.4). Volume panel show grows down from the top with a ≈12-epx overshoot, 217 ms (4.5.1). The timeout hide is instant (4.5.3). Pressing Start while it shows slides it up in ≈134 ms (4.5.5). Every timing is 14393; the 15063 cross-checks are low-refresh captures (LOW, 4.6), and the 15063 volume panel is UNMEASURED (4.5.8).

Findings that bear on phase scope (the full notes are at the end of each section's tally):
- **Phase 06, voicemail:** W10M had no voicemail list. Voicemail was an app-bar button that calls the mailbox (1.7.1, user guide). The visual voicemail list in build task 5 has no W10M reference and would be a new design in W10M style.
- **Phase 06, calls:** incoming call on the lock screen is a two-step flow: Slide up, then Answer / Text reply (canned SMS) / Remind me / Ignore (1.5). In-call adds Hold, Add call, Merge / Private (conference), Bluetooth routing and "End call & answer" for call waiting (1.5.8, 1.6.1). The phase lists none of these. "Video" was a Skype upgrade with no Android equivalent. Dial-pad and in-call rows scale with screen height, so the 19.5:9 S25 needs a scaling rule rather than copied constants (1.2.3).
- **Phase 06, Messaging:** the thread view, group threads and received MMS have no governing-build reference (2.4–2.5). They need an owner ruling or accepted approximations. The attach menu (location, contact, voice note, ringtone) is wider than the phase's "pictures, group texts, audio/video". Dual-SIM phones show a "Send on: <SIM>" selector (2.3.1).
- **Phase 09:** the "W10M-style Notebook" is a flat icon + label list, not a pivot or cards, and on 15063 it is a light page. Reminders and Settings are separate ≡ destinations, not Notebook sections (3.1, 3.3). The only measured editable template is a category settings form: title, description, toggle, bottom Save (3.4).
- **Phase 04:** the pull-down must be a finger-tracked drag with a fixed-duration settle and a progress-linked scrim, not a fixed animation fired by a swipe (4.1). On the source phones the open panel covered the nav bar, which an Android overlay may not be able to do (4.1.10). The volume panel's show overshoot and instant timeout hide are specific behaviours (4.5).

## 4. Action center and volume panel motion

Paths are relative to `$R7`. Frame folders `frames/a_<event>/f_NNNN.png` are native-fps crops; each folder's `INFO.txt` holds the start time and crop, so frame N is at t = start + N / fps. Per-frame tracks are in `data/a_*.tsv`: handle-bar rows, touch-indicator centroid, frame difference, and 40-px luminance bands. Scripts:
- `scripts/a_pipe.py` / `a_pipe2.py`: accent handle bar, touch dot, and bands.
- `a_vol.py`: extent of the grey volume panel.
- `a_tmpl.py`: vertical template offset.
- `a_hshift.py`: horizontal offset.
- `a_closefit.py`: content offset plus the panel/app boundary on close.
- `a_bezfit.py`: fits a CSS cubic-bezier to normalised progress and reports residuals.
- `a_series.py`, `a_show.py`: listings.

Positions are in video px on the stated crop and converted with the source ruler. "Open %" is the handle bottom as a fraction of screen height. Timing tolerance is ±1 frame, and **every source refreshes its picture less often than its frame rate**, as noted per source. R3 A19/A20 hold the static geometry and are not repeated here.

Implementation-relevant findings for phase 04:
- **(a) Finger tracking.** The action center follows the finger 1:1 and holds partial positions (4.1.2). Release then runs a fixed-duration animation of ≈290 ms. After a slow release this is a slow-start S-curve; after a flick the finger's speed carries on and slows in the last ≈100 ms (4.1.4–4.1.6). A stationary release at 27 % snaps closed, and an upward flick closes from any height (4.1.7, 4.2.2). A fixed open/close animation triggered on swipe would not match.
- **(b) Scrim.** The dim is tied to how far the panel is open, not to time (4.1.8), and in light theme it lightens toward white (4.1.11).
- **(c) Nav bar.** The open panel covers the nav bar on A1 and A3 (4.1.10); a phase-04 overlay may not be able to cover Android's nav bar.
- **(d) Volume panel.** Show overshoots by ≈12 epx and takes ≈217 ms (4.5.1). The timeout hide is instant (4.5.3), but navigating away slides the panel up in ≈134 ms (4.5.5).
- **(e) Notification swipe.** A swiped notification group dims to ≈0.4 past ≈20–30 % of width (4.4.2).
