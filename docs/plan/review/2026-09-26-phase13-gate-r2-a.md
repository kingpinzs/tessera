## Findings

### BLOCKING

**B1: product defect. The Start background's decode is held twice whenever a picture is set, acrylic on or off. `BackgroundDecoder`'s
"decoded once per process and URI" (`start/BackgroundDecoder.kt:12-14`) does not hold.**

How it happens:
- Start's `rememberBackground` keeps its own decode in `produceState` (`start/StartPage.kt:1146-1147`).
- `AppListBackdrop` keeps its own in `picture` (`ui/fluent/StaticBackdrop.kt:181`, `:190`).
- Both call `BackgroundDecoder.decode` in the same frame, whenever the URI changes and at process start (the pager composes both pages, T13-15).
- `decode` checks only the finished-decode cache (`:27`) and records nothing in flight. So both calls miss, each decodes, and each page holds its
  own bitmap for as long as the picture is set.
- With acrylic on, the static build's decode (`StaticBackdrop.kt:71`) starts a frame later (it waits for the page size). When it finishes last,
  the one-entry cache (`BackgroundDecoder.kt:48`) keeps it as a third copy.

The kept captures show two copies as the settled state, not a transient:
- **E10, acrylic off:** the picture costs B − A = 20 552 KB (`E10/E10.txt:23`, `:28`; 20 879 KB in `E10-run4-db078d6a/E10.txt:23`, `:28`).
  One decode of the 1080 × 2340 checker is 9 872 KB.
- **E10_DIAG, native heap:** M2 − M4 = 51 276 − 31 576 = 19 700 KB (`E10_DIAG/E10_DIAG.txt:24`, `:34`). Both states hold the Photos face,
  pushed after M1 at `scripts/e10_diag.sh:57`, so the difference is the picture alone. It equals 2 × 9 872 KB to within 44 KB.
- **The layer is counted once:** the 1080 × 2196 layer is 9 264 KB and is counted once: C − B = 9 514 KB (`E10/E10.txt:32`) and M3 − M2 native =
  9 444 KB. Bitmaps are counted once each, so B holds two decodes.
- **EDGE_HUGE:** B = 212 180 KB (`EDGE_HUGE/EDGE_HUGE.txt:18`). Two 4000² decodes (2 × 62 500 KB) account for its excess over E10's B; one
  would leave about 60 MB unexplained.

Why it blocks:
- It breaks the Decisions: static source (a) shares the decode ("through a `BackgroundDecoder` object both call"), and Memory has the layer
  built "from the already-sampled background decode", with the budget adding one layer.
- **P2 on the phone** (baseline + 30 MB with the layer built) will trip on it. At QHD+ the layer is ≈ 18 MB, and a second copy of a screen-size
  picture is another ≈ 18 MB (36 > 30). A 12 MP camera photo, which phase 01's sampling keeps whole (height ≤ 4800), adds ≈ 48 MB with the second copy.
- **Low-RAM devices**, where the rule turns acrylic off, still carry the second copy: it is the fallback's picture, not the layer.
- **Phase 14's pod bay** draws the same fallback picture (T14-11) and would become a third holder.
- **No row can see it:** E10 and EDGE_HUGE gate only C − B and A2 − A, and the duplicate cancels out of both.

Round 1 filed this as A-N1, "transiently 2–3 ×". The triage left it for Jeremy on that premise. The captures above show it is not transient.

Fix at the producer: `BackgroundDecoder` keeps one in-flight decode per URI (a `Deferred` every caller awaits), so all callers get the same
`ImageBitmap`. Re-verify under the specific-tests ruling:
- E10, with B − A recorded against one decode (≈ 9.9 MB at FHD+).
- EDGE_HUGE, E1, E2, E13 and BS_STEP (the static build path).
- Phase 01's Start-background rows (they drive the decode).
