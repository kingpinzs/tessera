# L13-14 / L13-15 fix: review round 2, reviewer B (evidence, adversarial), 2026-09-28

Reviewed: 74a7dcba (the round-2 fix), c8aae11e (the drivers, the drv3 runs, the triage and the ledger), and the three
APKs' dex (dexdump 36.0.0, in scratch). Read-only: no build and no adb. I did not read the other round-2 report.

**Verdict: FAIL.** The evidence holds: each build is the commit it is named for, (a2) fails only on 9e658a94, and
L13-15 (b) now fails on ce79bc17 from a clean page with the pressed city's menu. One blocking finding, by code reading:
for about one frame after the bar collapses, the bar has no target and the scrim is already gone.

## Blocking findings

1. **In the frame after a collapse, a touch on the bar reaches the row under it again** (`clock/ClockWidgets.kt:171`,
   with `ui/components/ModalOverlay.kt:41-63` and `clock/ClockActivity.kt:165`, `:214`).
   - Back closes "…" through `dismissOverlay`. That applies `barExpanded = false` at once, so the OverlayLayer drops the
     scrim at the next layout pass. Compose runs that pass before it hit-tests each touch (`ModalOverlay.kt:27-29`).
   - The bar's target is chosen in composition (`val expanded = isExpanded()`, then `.then(if (expanded) Modifier else
     …pointerInput…)`). Composition runs only at the next frame. Between the two, the scrim is not placed and the bar
     is still composed as expanded, with no target.
   - Failure scenario: on the Alarm tab with the row's 8 alarms, tap "…" and press Back. Then put a finger down at
     (60, 2124) before the Clock's next frame. The down passes the bar's blank part and reaches the 7th alarm row
     ([0,1873][1080,2137]). A tap opens that alarm's editor; a hold opens its hold menu, hidden under the bar. That is
     L13-14 as found. On 4a1b7841 the same down lands on the always-on target.
   - A scrim tap, a menu item and a tab change also collapse the bar (a normal write, the same frame). The window is
     narrow. It is the Back-to-recomposition gap that L13-3 measured at 7-17 ms and hit with its harness
     (5 of 70 holds lost at about 4 ms after Back). Jeremy had that race fixed, so by this project's own standard it is
     a real defect.
   - The commit's premise, "expanded, the scrim already covers the list", is false for that frame. Fix direction:
     choose the target at placement, as the scrim is, by reading `isExpanded()` (e.g. a target layer placed only while
     collapsed); prove it with L13-3's window probe, a down about 4 ms after Back.

## Notes (not blocking)

1. **What the force-stops hide: the ring.** Each ring-launcher.txt starts at the last force-stop (L13_14: the restore
   only; L13_15: 2 s, no case), so no case has an app log; the final force-stop was already in drv1. Nothing a case
   needs is lost: the seeds are on disk, and the alarms re-arm at process start ("rearm (process start): 8 alarms").
2. **No case touches the collapsed bar after an expand.** (a) runs on a bar that was never expanded, and (a2) ends in
   a force-stop. So the target coming back after a collapse (the state 74a7dcba creates) is code reading. Re-running
   (a) right after (a2), in the same activity, would cover it, and a fast variant would probe finding 1.
3. **(a2)'s "no editor" assertion cannot fail** on any of the three builds, since the scrim lies under the tap on all
   of them. (a2) also has no "row under the point" assertion, though the dumps show row 7 under (60,2094). The ledger's
   "the row asserts a row lies under each touch point" no longer holds for (a2).
4. **Code reading only:** collapsing by "…", Back, a menu item or a tab change; the expanded bar on the other tabs and
   in the editors; the other buttons and the chevrons; the release after a hold and the anchor after a scroll; a
   settle past a quarter of the width on the fix build.
5. **Expanding removes the bar's pointerInput node**, which is an ancestor of the buttons' clickables in the hit path.
   So a press held on another bar button by a second finger while "…" expands is cancelled (HitPathTracker cancels a
   detached node's subtree; L13-3 diag2). This is multi-touch only.
6. **L13-16's line reference is stale:** ClockWidgets.kt:410-411 (RowHoldMenu) is :412-413 at 74a7dcba and HEAD.
