## Checked (holds)

1. **The rule.** The scrim is hit only while it is composed (`expanded`, read at :136, used at :142) and placed (the
   outer layer reads `isExpanded()` at placement, :140 and ModalOverlay.kt:50). The target is placed when
   `!expanded || !isExpanded()` (:173) is true at its own placement. Both layers read one state (`nav.barExpanded`) in
   the same phase, so an apply invalidates both, and the relayout before each hit test (L13-3's rule) re-places both in
   one pass. They can only disagree if one of them is laid out without the other. Neither reads anything else at
   placement, and their sizes change only on a recomposition.
2. **Frame by frame.**
   - Steady collapsed: no scrim is composed; the target is placed. Steady expanded: the scrim is composed and placed;
     the target is not placed. (a2) passes on cb8a791b, so the unplaced target is not hit.
   - The frame after "…" expands the bar (a plain write): the composition is still collapsed, so no scrim exists and
     `!expanded` keeps the target placed, before and after the apply. At the recomposition, the scrim is composed and
     the target gets a new `active`, so a new measure policy. Both are laid out in the same pass.
   - The frame after Back (`dismissOverlay`, ClockActivity.kt:214): the target's last placement ran while it was
     composed as expanded, so it read `isExpanded()` and was invalidated along with the scrim layer. The scrim is
     unplaced and the target is placed. Suppose Back lands after the expand's recomposition but before its layout. The
     target's pending relayout then runs the new policy, which reads the new value. Only (a3) runs this frame: 0 of 30
     leaks on cb8a791b, 6 of 30 on b8364831.
   - Closes that use a plain write: the scrim tap (:144), "…" (:180), a menu item (:156), a header tab (`tapTab` →
     `leaveTabModes`, ClockActivity.kt:120/142, over the scrim's Box, not under it). Until the apply, both layers are
     stale together: the scrim is hit, the target is not placed, and a bar tap re-closes an already-closed menu. After
     the apply, both flip together. At the recomposition, the scrim leaves and `active` becomes `{ true }`. No frame is
     left with neither of them.
   - The short-circuit `!expanded ||` means a collapsed-composed target never reads `isExpanded()`. That is harmless,
     because its value is true either way.
3. **Sibling, not ancestor.** Hit testing tries the Row before the target, which is its earlier sibling.
   - A down on a button stops there, so the target is never in a button's path, and its placing or unplacing cannot
     cancel a press (round 2's multi-touch note is closed).
   - A down on the blank part misses the Row, which has no pointer node, and lands on the target. The target consumes
     nothing, and the bar Box shares nothing with its siblings, so the list under the bar never sees that touch.
   - The buttons cover the whole Row (fillMaxHeight, 68 and 48 dp wide, bar ≥ 48 dp). No minimum-touch-target
     expansion reaches the blank part.
   - Each button's hit path is now the same as on ce79bc17.
4. **Size.**
   - `matchParentSize` has the Box measure the Row first, then the layer at the bar's fixed size, 48 or 68 dp by
     full width. The layer places its fillMaxSize child at (0, 0), so it covers the whole bar in both states.
   - In the frame after Back, the bar is still composed at 68 dp, and the target covers all of that drawn bar.
   - A hold that starts in its top 20 dp keeps the target after the bar shrinks, because the hit path is fixed at the
     down.
5. **The same split elsewhere in the Clock:** none that leaks.
   - The hold menus, the editor flyouts and the city search are OverlayLayer plus composed content. Their "not hit" is
     meant to expose the page, so no target has to complement them.
   - CompareStrip's target is unconditional. Its swap with the bar is composition-only, and a target covers the bar's
     48 dp in every frame on both sides of the swap.
   - Only the "…" handler below reads the same stale value, and it leaks nothing.
6. **Scope.** One file, the bar only. The Row, the buttons, the scrim, the menu and CompareStrip are unchanged.
