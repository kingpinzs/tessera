## Verdict: FAIL

## Blocking findings

1. **In the frame after the bar collapses, the bar has no target, and L13-14 is back** (clock/ClockWidgets.kt:136, :140, :171).
   - The same state is read in two phases. Composition decides the target (`val expanded = isExpanded()` at :136, used at
     :171). OverlayLayer decides the scrim under the bar, and it reads `isExpanded()` while placing (:140; ModalOverlay.kt:50).
   - Layout sees a collapse before composition does:
     - Back applies its write at once (ClockActivity.kt:214, `dismissOverlay` → `Snapshot.sendApplyNotifications`).
     - Compose lays out before it hit-tests each touch. In ui-android 1.11.4, `handleMotionEvent` calls `measureAndLayout`
       first.
     - Recomposition waits for the next frame.
   - So, for up to one frame, the scrim is unplaced while the bar is still composed as expanded: 68 dp tall, no target.
     A down on the bar's blank part hits nothing in the bar and skips the unplaced OverlayLayer. It reaches the pager and
     the row under it. The recomposition adds the target, but the down's hit path is already fixed, so the row still gets
     the up.
   - Failure scenario: L13-14 (a)'s seed and point, with L13-11's timing.
     - Setup: the Alarm tab with 8 alarms; open "…".
     - Send `input keyevent 4 & sleep 0.0N; input tap 60 2124` from one device shell (l13_11_row.sh:65's form).
     - When the tap lands before the next frame, it opens the editor of the row under the point (a3aa556a2, "L1314 7",
       in drv3). A hold there opens that row's menu hidden under the bar, L13-14's other symptom.
     - On 9e658a94 the always-on target took this touch. The window is reachable on the emulator: L13-11's before run
       (a7c9d715) sent taps this way and lost 7 of 30 to the stale scrim, so 7 of 30 landed before the recomposition.
   - Every other collapse opens the same window: a tap on the scrim, "…", a header tab (`leaveTabModes`), a menu item.
     Those writes are plain, so they apply at the next main-thread message and last until the frame. Both editors share
     ClockAppBar (AlarmTab.kt:266, TimerTab.kt:260), so the window exists there too.
   - The expand frame is safe. The bar keeps its target until the same recomposition that composes the scrim.
   - The premise "expanded, the scrim already covers the list" holds between frames, not in this one; drv3's (a2) taps
     0.8 s after the change.
   - A direction, not a prescription: the bar may lack a target only while the scrim is both composed and placed. One way:
     while the bar is composed as expanded, keep a target under the buttons in `OverlayLayer(active = { !isExpanded() })`
     inside the bar. That layer is placed in the same layout pass that unplaces the scrim. As a sibling under the buttons,
     not an ancestor modifier, it also stays out of the buttons' hit paths (note 1).

## Checked (holds)
- **Steady state.**
  - Collapsed: the target takes the blank part, and the list is never in that touch's path.
  - Expanded: the bar has no pointer node. A blank-part tap reaches `clock_bar_scrim` (:144-145) and closes the menu.
    Drv3 (a2) fails only on 9e658a94.
  - b8364831's dex is 74a7dcba: `if-eqz` on `expanded` gives `Modifier.Companion`, else `pointerInput(Unit, ClockAppBar$2$1.INSTANCE)`.
- **A touch already down on the blank part when the bar changes.**
  - Collapsed to expanded: the target detaches. ui 1.11.4 removes its node from the hit path and cancels it
    (`NodeParent.removePointerInputModifierNode` → `Node.dispatchCancel`). Compose hit-tests only on a down, so that
    pointer then reaches neither the list nor the new scrim.
  - Expanded to collapsed: the scrim is unplaced, then removed and cancelled, and the new target is not in the path.
    No leak.
- **The buttons, single touch.**
  - The chain changes only after "…"'s up (or after another write), so no button is mid-gesture when the node comes or goes.
  - The buttons are the bar's children, and their own modifier chains do not change.
  - The handler is a non-capturing singleton, remembered in its own group. Adding it again starts a fresh, idle node.
- **The compare strip.** 74a7dcba does not touch it. It takes the bar's place, so no "…" scrim lies under it.

## Notes
1. **Multi-touch, not recorded: an ancestor that detaches cancels the press under it.**
   - Scenario: one finger holds a bar button ("+", Select, Compare) while a second finger taps "…".
   - The expand detaches the bar's target. ui 1.11.4 then cancels the target's whole subtree in the hit path, the held
     button included, so the button's lift runs nothing.
   - On ce79bc17 and 9e658a94 that lift ran it ("+" opened the editor with `nav.barExpanded` still true). Arguably
     better, not a defect; finding 1's suggested shape avoids it anyway.
2. **The triage's "recorded, not fixed" items are out of scope, as recorded.**
   - **Hold menus and flyouts no longer close on a tap on the bar's blank part or the strip's centre.** This is the "…"
     case's mechanism. But the bar is not the page, and every close path users rely on remains: a tap on the page, Back,
     the verb, and the bar's buttons (L13-13).
     - It also removes a hazard. On ce79bc17, a tap on the bar over a menu hidden under it (L13-16's shape) could land on
       that menu's Remove.
     - ClockFlyout's doc still says "A tap outside closes it" (ClockWidgets.kt:219), and the bar is now an exception. The
       ledger records this, and the owner should decide.
   - **A swipe on the bar no longer turns tabs:** it comes with the target and matches the header band. **L13-16:** a
     separate, older cause (the anchor). **The notch, the "…" spacer, coverage, line refs:** fine as recorded.
3. **The ledger.** L13-14's row says "the bar is a target only while collapsed". Once finding 1 is fixed, the row should
   name the frame rule it follows (placement, like the scrim), and the row should gain a Back-plus-tap case on the bar's
   blank part in L13-11's form, which fails on 74a7dcba.
