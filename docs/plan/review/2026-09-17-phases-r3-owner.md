- R3D-02 / R3T-B7, phase 03: C. Places can be saved both ways: "Save current location" / "this is home" at the place (GPS only, no internet, no Google), or by typing an address, looked up once through OpenStreetMap's Nominatim search (not Google, P5). The lookup is an internet use only at the moment a place is saved (amends A11). Places live in Cortana's Settings (a Places page) and are reusable by place reminders. Jeremy: "C"
- R3T-B6, phase 03: A. A person reminder fires on any real contact with that person in either direction: an outgoing call, an incoming call that is answered, or a text sent or received. A missed or declined call doesn't count. Jeremy: "A"
