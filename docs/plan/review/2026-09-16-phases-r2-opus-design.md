## Part 2 — New findings

### BLOCKING

#### R2D-01 — BLOCKING — Phase 07 — glance activation cancels Samsung's delayed auto-lock, leaving an unlocked phone behind glance
- **Claim:** phase-07 L11-13 Goal "never weakens the real secure lock screen underneath"; L22 "glance is a `showWhenLocked` + `turnScreenOn` activity started on the screen-off event"; E4 L44 "With a lock set (`adb shell locksettings set-pin 1234`) then `input keyevent KEYCODE_SLEEP` … every tap, swipe, Home and Back from glance lands on the keyguard".
- **Evidence:** One UI's "Auto lock when screen turns off" runs from Immediately up to 30 min (https://www.sammobile.com/news/how-do-you-automatically-lock-your-screen/). On AOSP, a screen-timeout sleep does not lock at once: KeyguardViewMediator schedules the lock after the lock-after-timeout delay (`doKeyguardLaterLocked`), and when the screen turns back on `cancelDoKeyguardLaterLocked()` cancels the pending lock (https://github.com/aosp-mirror/platform_frameworks_base/blob/master/packages/SystemUI/src/com/android/systemui/keyguard/KeyguardViewMediator.java). Glance turns the screen on within milliseconds of screen-off, so with any delay other than Immediately the keyguard never engages. Glance then shows over an unlocked phone, and the edge rule L55 "Touches on glance do nothing except wake to the keyguard" has no keyguard to wake to: the next touch lands on Start or the last app. E4 cannot catch this. A `KEYCODE_SLEEP` sleep (like a power-key sleep with "lock instantly" on) takes the lock-immediately branch, not the timeout branch. Yet screen timeout is the most common way glance starts.
- **Fix:** decide in Decisions: when `KeyguardManager.isDeviceSecure()`, glance starts only after `isKeyguardLocked()` is true. The screen stays off until the lock engages, so glance shows up to the lock delay late. Don't lock with `DevicePolicyManager.lockNow()`: it sets strong-auth-required, which kills fingerprint unlock and breaks P2. Add an E-row with `locksettings set-pin 1234`, `settings put secure lock_screen_lock_after_timeout 5000` and `settings put system screen_off_timeout 15000`. Let the screen time out; glance must not be visible until `dumpsys window` / `dumpsys trust` shows the keyguard locked, and a tap on glance must land on the keyguard. Add a phone P-row with "Auto lock when screen turns off" at 5 s and at 30 s. Add an edge for power-key sleep with "Lock instantly with side key" off.

#### R2D-02 — BLOCKING — Phase 02 — the reflow approximation and the folder-create rule contradict each other, so E3 cannot pass
- **Claim:** phase-02 L31 "Reflow while dragging over occupied cells … approximation, as soon as the dragged tile's centre enters an occupied cell, the tiles there slide out of the way down the grid"; L34 "while a dragged tile hovers over another tile, the target's icon shrinks into a mini tile … and the target stays highlighted behind the dragged tile; on release the dragged tile shrinks into the target"; E3 L55 "Dropping a tile on a tile creates a folder".
- **Evidence:** under L31 the target tile slides away the moment the dragged tile's centre reaches its cell, so there is never a tile under the finger to hover over or drop on. L34 and E3 require the target to stay put. The two were applied from separate R6 rows: §1.3.3 is UNMEASURED and §1.6.1 is LOW, from a 2015 recording. Nothing reconciles them.
- **Fix (agent call, no owner question):** one disambiguation rule, tagged "approximation" and judged in H5/H10. For example: the centre over the inner ≈60 % of an occupied tile shows the folder preview (§1.6.1), while crossing its outer band, or a dwell of ≈X ms without a drop, reflows it (L31). Or: reflow happens only on release into an occupied cell's edge band. Rewrite L31, L34 and E3 against the chosen rule, and add an E-row that exercises both paths in one drag.

#### R2D-03 — MAJOR — Phase 01 — the "last resumed activity" rule for Back on Start has no exclusions and no boot rule; the E20 reboot row likely fails
- **Claim:** phase-01 L83 "finds the last activity resumed since boot other than Start itself (`UsageStatsManager.queryEvents`, `ACTIVITY_RESUMED`)"; L84 "Back history resets when the screen locks"; E20 L163 "right after `adb reboot` (no other activity resumed since boot) Back leaves Start showing (X12)".
- **Evidence:** the only exclusion is "Start itself". During boot, AOSP resumes `com.android.settings/.FallbackHome` (a HOME activity) until the user is unlocked. Samsung builds likewise resume system activities before the shell. Whether these reach UsageStats after unlock is not proven either way. If they do, Back right after reboot relaunches Settings and E20 fails. Other activities resume routinely and are not excluded either: other HOME activities (One UI Home as the crash fallback per the phase-01 L209 edge), permission controller, package installer, resolver/chooser, the phase 07 glance activity (it resumes after every lock, so after unlock Back on Start would try to "resume" glance), and the phase 06 in-call UI. "Resets when the screen locks" doesn't say whether the trigger is screen-off or an actual keyguard lock. With a Samsung lock delay these differ. R6 D1 says "since the last time your screen was locked".
- **Fix:** the history window starts at the later of the last keyguard-locked transition and `ACTION_USER_UNLOCKED`. A candidate must have a launcher activity for its user and must not resolve for `CATEGORY_HOME`, SystemUI, permission controller, installer, resolver or an IME. The shell's own non-launcher activities (glance in 07) are excluded, and phase 07 states that ADD. E20: after reboot and unlock, capture `dumpsys usagestats` and assert that Back still leaves Start showing.

#### R2D-04 — MAJOR — Phase 03 / 08 — the model-storage decision contradicts sherpa-onnx's Android path, and speech-model RAM lands in the Home process
- **Claim:** phase-03 L40 "models stay stored uncompressed in the APK … and are memory-mapped from the APK, never copied to files (no double storage); both verified at build start"; phase-08 P2 L47 "the launcher process unchanged from phase 01's figure".
- **Evidence:** espeak-ng opens its data by filesystem path. sherpa-onnx's own Android TTS app copies it first: `copyDataDir(dataDir)` → `copyAssets(dataDir)` → `application.getExternalFilesDir(null)` (https://raw.githubusercontent.com/k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx/master/android/SherpaOnnxTts/app/src/main/java/com/k2fsa/sherpa/onnx/MainActivity.kt). sherpa-onnx's asset loader doesn't mmap either; it copies into a heap buffer: `std::vector<char> buffer(p, p + asset_length);` (https://raw.githubusercontent.com/k2-fsa/sherpa-onnx/master/sherpa-onnx/csrc/file-utils.cc). So ASR and TTS weights take their full size in RAM in whatever process loads them. No doc names that process. By default the VoiceInteractionService and the IME (phase 05 voice typing) run in the main process, the one holding HOME, the listener and the tile engine: the exposure F1-M8 fixed for the LLM. Phase 08 P2's "unchanged from phase 01's figure" then fails by construction once phase 03 lands.
- **Fix:** rewrite L40. espeak-ng-data is extracted once into app-private storage (versioned, checksummed) and models are read into native buffers. Record the RAM budget. Name the process: speech engines in their own `:speech` process, bound by the Cortana session and the IME, or loaded per session and unloaded after an idle timeout. Add the edge "speech process killed → Start unaffected". Phase 08 P2's baseline becomes "the launcher process unchanged from the previous phase's figure".

#### R2D-08 — MAJOR — Phase 07 — the activation mechanism has no way to turn glance off and re-triggers on its own screen-offs
- **Claim:** phase-07 L20 modes "always on / 15 min / 30 s after leaving a pocket via proximity / off"; L22 activation "on the screen-off event"; E5 L45 "`adb emu sensor set proximity 0` (covered) turns glance off"; L54 "Power key pressed while glance is showing: the screen really sleeps, glance does not re-trigger in a loop"; L24 "hard ceiling for always-on = 2 % per hour".
- **Evidence:** glance is an ordinary activity on an AWAKE display. It must hold the screen on (keep-screen-on) or the normal screen timeout sleeps it. Every way it ends (15 min / 30 s mode expiry, proximity covered, screen timeout) produces a screen-off event, and the one activation rule then starts glance again: an on/off loop. The edge covers only the power key. An app can't put the display to sleep on its own. The options are `DevicePolicyManager.lockNow()` (strong-auth side effect, see R2D-01), accessibility `GLOBAL_ACTION_LOCK_SCREEN` (phase 04's service; depends-on is `[01]`), or releasing keep-screen-on and waiting out the timeout. A third-party app can't enter the display's DOZE state (only the system doze component can), so always-on keeps the SoC out of suspend. The 2 %/h ceiling is not proven either way, and the doc doesn't say what happens if P1 fails.
- **Fix:** Decisions name (1) the screen-hold mechanism, (2) the turn-off mechanism per mode, with depends-on updated if it uses phase 04's service, and (3) a re-trigger guard: a screen-off caused by glance ending, by proximity, or by power key while glance is on top starts no glance until the next wake. Add an E-row: 30 s pocket mode, uncover, and after 30 s the screen stays off (`dumpsys power` wakefulness Asleep for ≥ 60 s). If always-on exceeds the ceiling, Jeremy is re-asked (it's his ruled mode); no silent drop.
