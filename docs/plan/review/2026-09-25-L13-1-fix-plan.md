# L13-1 — fix plan (a photo picked from inside Tess's window)

Owner part: phase 03 (Cortana: the reminder confirm card, the Reminders / reminder pages, the session window). Found by
phase 13's build at E3's fixture; blocks phase 13 (resume point on the ledger row: E3's fixture, `reminders_setup`).
Planned with Jeremy 2026-09-25 in the Stage A question shape (main checkout, emulator-5554).

## The defect

- **Crash.** `ReminderDetailPage.kt:100` calls `rememberLauncherForActivityResult(PickVisualMedia())`. The page is composed
  inside `CortanaSession` — a `VoiceInteractionSession`, not an Activity — whose view tree carries lifecycle, view-model
  and saved-state owners but no `ActivityResultRegistryOwner`, so composing the page throws
  `IllegalStateException: No ActivityResultRegistryOwner was provided via LocalActivityResultRegistryOwner`. Every route
  to the page composes it: the Reminders page's +, a Reminders row tap, a History row tap.
- **No-op.** `ResponseCardView`'s "Add a photo" row sends `CardAction.PICK_PHOTO`; `CortanaModel.onCardAction` leaves it
  to "the session" (`CortanaModel.kt:351`) and no session code handles it. `Card.photoUri` exists but nothing sets or
  draws it, and `Pending.SaveReminder` / `Pending.AwaitReminderTime` carry the draft `Reminder` whose `photoUri` is
  never filled from the card.
- Spec (phase 03 Decisions, the R6 §3.4.2 card line): "the photo is chosen with Android's photo picker
  (`MediaStore.ACTION_PICK_IMAGES`) and shown on the reminder notification"; the reminder page's camera "enables while no
  photo is attached" (H25).
- Evidence: qa/phase-13/L13-1-pre13-1ee619f8/, qa/phase-13/L13-1-phase13-365bd248/ (driver
  qa/phase-13/scripts/l13_1_repro.sh). Note: the 365bd248 run's "pid changed" check failed (22290 before and after)
  although the crash was logged at that pid; the regression row counts crash-buffer entries, not pids.

## Constraints the fix works inside (existing code and Decisions, not new rulings)

- Tess's window sits above every activity: MICPERM run 1 caught Android's permission prompt resumed but hidden under the
  session, even started as an assistant activity (`CortanaSession.requestMicrophone`). The picker cannot show over her.
- Closing Tess drops the pending card: `CortanaModel.stop()` on `onHide` resets the state (H12, "a pending request never
  survives the session"). So Tess steps aside without closing while the picker is up.
- Precedent: `CortanaPermissionActivity` and `CortanaUnlockActivity` + `UnlockBridge` are the shell's existing
  "a session is not an Activity, this is the smallest thing that is" pass-throughs.

## Rulings

- **Q1 (2026-09-25, Jeremy: "(b)") — how a photo is picked from inside Tess's window: give the session a real
  `ActivityResultRegistryOwner`.** Its registry's launches go through a general pass-through activity that runs the
  contract's intent and hands the result back to the registry, so `ReminderDetailPage`'s existing
  `rememberLauncherForActivityResult` works unchanged, as does any later launcher composed inside Tess. The card's Add a
  photo uses the same registry. Tess steps aside (without closing) while the pass-through's target is up. Rejected:
  (a) a photo-only trampoline + bridge with the page's launcher removed; (c) moving the reminder pages out of Tess.
- **Q2 (2026-09-25, Jeremy: "(b)") — how a reminder keeps its photo: a private copy.** At pick time the photo is copied
  into the launcher's own storage, and the reminder's `photoUri` points at the copy, so the photo survives the gallery
  original being deleted or moved and needs no lasting grant on the picker's URI. Derived (agent, from the code): there is
  no "clear History" action (History rows are removed only by the long-press Delete, `store.delete`), so a copy is deleted
  when its reminder is deleted from the store, and a copy nothing saved is deleted when its card is cancelled or its
  unsaved page is left. No migration: no product route has ever attached a photo (this defect), and phase 03's E15
  fixture makes no photo reminder (phase 13 r3 triage §3). Rejected: (a) a persistable grant on the gallery URI;
  (c) grant + display copy.
- **Q3 (2026-09-25, Jeremy: "(a)", after seeing the card, qa/phase-13/L13-1-phase13-365bd248/card-after.png) — what the
  confirm card shows once a photo is picked: the photo takes the "Add a photo" row's place**, full card width at the
  reminder page's proportions (aspect 1.78, the card's 16-epx side margins); a tap on it picks a different photo (the
  replaced copy is deleted); Remind / Cancel and the callout move down below it. The saved-reminder card ("I'll remind
  you.") stays as R7 §3.8.1 measured it; the photo shows on the Reminders row, the reminder page and the notification. P4
  design with no R6 source (R6 §3.4.2 / §3.4.3 show only the empty row): a NEEDS-HUMAN row for Jeremy. Applies to every
  card with the row (the time card, the "When would you like to be reminded?" card, the place and person cards).
  Rejected: (b) a thumbnail in the row, "Change photo"; (c) no change on the card.

## The fix (agent, from Q1–Q3)

1. `CortanaSession` becomes an `ActivityResultRegistryOwner` and provides itself through
   `LocalActivityResultRegistryOwner` around `CortanaSessionRoot` (activity-compose 1.13 looks the owner up in the
   composition local, then walks `LocalContext` — a `VoiceInteractionSessionService`, never an owner — so the provider is
   what makes it visible). Its registry's `onLaunch` asks the contract for a synchronous result first, else builds the
   contract's intent and starts a new pass-through activity, then steps Tess aside with `setUiEnabled(false)` (the session
   stays shown: no `onHide`, so no `model.stop()`, and the card, the route and a page's typed draft all survive — H12
   holds). The result comes back through a bridge keyed by request code to `dispatchResult`, and Tess comes back with
   `setUiEnabled(true)`. Back from the picker = RESULT_CANCELED = Tess back unchanged.
2. The pass-through (`CortanaResultActivity`): not exported, translucent, excluded from recents, its own task affinity —
   the `CortanaPermissionActivity` / `CortanaUnlockActivity` pattern. It launches the intent it was handed with
   `StartActivityForResult`, delivers (request code, result code, data) to the bridge, and finishes; a target that does
   not resolve delivers RESULT_CANCELED. Not exported, so no other app can make the launcher start an intent for it.
3. `ReminderPhotos` owns the private copies (Q2): `adopt(uri)` copies a picked photo into
   `filesDir/reminder_photos/<uuid>` and returns the copy's `file:` URI (`openInputStream` reads it, so
   `rememberReminderPhoto` and the notification in `ReminderScheduler` need no change); `release(uri)` deletes a copy
   (only ever inside that folder). The copy is made at pick time, while the picker's read grant is live.
   `ReminderStore.delete` releases the reminder's copy; the store's load deletes copies no reminder references (a process
   death between pick and save).
4. The reminder page keeps its `rememberLauncherForActivityResult`; its callback adopts the photo, and a picked copy
   that is never saved is released when the page leaves the composition.
5. The card: the host of `ResponseCardView` registers the same picker launcher and launches it on `PICK_PHOTO`; its result
   goes to `CortanaModel.attachPhoto`, which adopts the photo into the pending reminder's draft
   (`Pending.SaveReminder` / `Pending.AwaitReminderTime`) and the card's `photoUri`; a replaced copy is released, and a
   pending draft's copy is released when the card is cancelled, replaced by another request, or the session closes
   (`model.stop()`). `ResponseCardView` draws `card.photoUri` in the row's place (Q3), tagged `cortana_card_photo`.

## Found at build (agent, 2026-09-25)

- **Tess goes quiet when she steps aside.** The first fix build (apk 20c0d593, row L13-1-row-fix-20c0d593: 8 failures)
  showed her question's speech running on under the picker and then "question spoken; listening for the answer" 5 s
  after the picker closed: with other timing the microphone would open behind the picker, and the listening layout
  took the row's next tap. `CortanaModel.onSteppedAside` (called by the registry's step-aside) stops speech and
  listening and cancels the listen-after; the card and whatever is pending stay, and she comes back quiet.
- **The not-exported check runs as the shell uid.** adbd runs as root on this emulator and root may start any activity,
  so `adb shell am start` proved nothing; the row starts the pass-through with `su 2000 am start`, which Android refuses
  for a non-exported activity ("not exported from uid 10150"), as it refuses `CortanaPermissionActivity` (probed).
