## The bar's buttons, "…" and the strip's chevrons (a finger's movement)
- The bar and the strip are siblings of the pager inside ClockScaffold's content Box (ClockTabs.kt:103-106, 161-164),
  not its descendants. A touch on a button hits the button's `clickable`, and the hit test stops there. The pager and
  the list were never in a button's hit path, and they still are not.
- The only change to a button's path is a new ancestor: `awaitEachGesture { awaitFirstDown(requireUnconsumed = false) }`
  (ClockWidgets.kt:169, WorldClockTab.kt:279). In foundation 1.11.4, `awaitFirstDown` and every `ForEachGestureKt` class
  make no `consume` call. The handler runs in Main after the button and consumes nothing in Initial, Main or Final.
- So a button's tap, a movement inside it, and a slide off it (cancelled by its own out-of-bounds check) all behave as
  they did on ce79bc17. This holds for the tab bar's buttons, "…" (its toggle included) and the chevrons.
- On the device, only Compare ran on the fix build (L13_14 (b), a still adb tap). "…", the chevrons and any finger
  movement on a button rest on this code reading.

## L13-15: CityRow on detectTapOrHold (ClockWidgets.kt:383-401, WorldClockTab.kt:195)
- **Scroll and tab swipe.** The list's scrollable and the pager's `detectHorizontalDragGestures` both consume in Main
  at slop; the pager's consume is in its slop lambda (bytecode). CityRow is their descendant, so it sees the consumed
  change in `waitForUpOrCancellation`'s Final-pass check (bytecode: `isConsumed`, then `awaitPointerEvent(Final)`, then
  `isConsumed`). That sets `ended = true`, so neither branch fires. The pointer-scope timeout is posted behind input,
  so it cannot land between the passes of one event.
- **Hold kept.** The hold fires only when `withTimeoutOrNull` expires with the gesture unended. That is the old
  "finger still down" case, minus the cancelled press.
- **Release kept.** After the hold, CityRow consumes the release in Initial, and every move too. The list and the pager
  (ancestors that act in Main) see only consumed changes. So nothing can scroll or swipe under an open menu. The old
  code let a scroll continue under it, which moved the row away from the anchor.
- **Anchor kept.** `onHold(top)` reads the same remembered `top` (`onGloballyPositioned`), and the `pointerInput(id)`
  key is unchanged.
- **Tap.** A tap now consumes its up (`onTap = {}`). This is harmless: the only ancestors are the list and the pager,
  and both act only past slop. The timer blocks already work this way.

## Notes (none blocking)
1. **While a menu is open, the bar's blank part no longer closes it.** This is a behaviour change, and neither the
   commit nor the ledger row records it.
   - The bar is drawn above the "…" scrim (the content Box's order: pager, OverlayLayer, bar).
   - On ce79bc17, a tap on the expanded bar's blank left part fell through to `clock_bar_scrim` and closed the menu. Now
     it lands on the bar and does nothing, and the menu stays open.
   - The menu is visible, and "…", a tap above the bar, Back and an item still close it.
   - The same happens with a row's hold menu (Alarm, Timer, World Clock) and with the alarm editor's flyouts: a tap on
     the bar's blank part or the strip's centre used to reach their scrim.
   - While the bar is expanded, its scrim already covered the list. So the new hit target is not needed then, and in
     that state its effect lies outside the defect. It also matches the scrim's own comment: "a tap anywhere *above*
     the bar".
   - No spec row settles it. Owner's call. To restore the old "…" behaviour, install the handler only while
     `!expanded` (ClockWidgets.kt:169).
2. **Also not recorded:** a swipe or vertical drag that starts on the bar's blank part or the strip's centre no longer
   turns tabs or scrolls the list. On ce79bc17 it did, because it fell through. This is consistent with the header
   band, which never swiped.
3. **New, pre-existing, outside L13-14's cause: the hidden hold menu is still reachable.**
   - A row's menu opens at the row's top + 60 dp and is 60 dp tall (WorldClockTab.kt:153; RowHoldMenu,
     ClockWidgets.kt:409).
   - A hold on a row whose visible part above the bar is 60 dp or less therefore opens a menu wholly under the bar and
     the nav bar. Its transparent scrim then takes the next tap.
   - This is the symptom in L13-14's row, reached from a visible row instead of from the bar. The fix does not claim to
     close it, and R7 §3.6.2 places the menu above the touch point. It could be its own ledger row.
4. **Either shape elsewhere in the Clock.**
   - **Pass-through.** ClockAppBar is shared by the tabs screen and both editors, so all three are covered. What is
     left:
     - The strip's notch (a 14 × 6 dp Canvas in the 54-dp outer Box, WorldClockTab.kt:269-274) passes a hold to the
       row under it. This is negligible.
     - The "…" menu's 8-dp top spacer and bottom padding (ClockWidgets.kt:150-154) pass a tap to its scrim, which
       closes the menu. ClockFlyout guards against this (ClockWidgets.kt:230); this menu does not. It is pre-existing,
       and the touch does not reach a list.
     - The header band, the status and nav bars, and the expanded screens' button boxes are not over a list.
   - **Null read as a hold.** No other site in the Clock has it: every `withTimeoutOrNull(Edit.HOLD_MS)` in the Clock
     is inside detectTapOrHold. Outside the Clock, the Recorder, App list and Calculator detectors record the cancel.
5. **Why L13-15 (b) cannot discriminate (code reading).** The 700-px swipe passes a quarter of the width, so the pager
   settles on Timer. Both builds' dumps show "timer page yes". `settleTab` takes the World Clock page, and its

   opened at slop is gone before the dump. A sideways drag past slop but under a quarter of the width (the pager
   springs back) would discriminate. Until then, the tab-swipe half of L13-15 rests on the code reading above.
