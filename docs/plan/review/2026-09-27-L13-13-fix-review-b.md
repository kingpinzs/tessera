## Blocking findings

**B1. The city search's result rows: nothing shows them working on bd114d21, and the code predicts they break under a finger.**
- Paths: `clock/WorldClockTab.kt:233` puts `modalOverlay` on the search page, and `:253` makes each result a `PressBox`.
  `clock/ClockWidgets.kt:193-203`: `PressBox` ends on `waitForUpOrCancellation`. `ui/components/ModalOverlay.kt:103-105`
  is the repo's own KDoc: the root "consumes every move, so that helper would end every item's press on the first move".
- How it breaks (checked in the bytecode of the foundation version the app resolves, 1.11.4; 1.12.1 is the same):
  - `waitForUpOrCancellation` reads each non-up event on Main, then waits for Final and returns null if any change is
    consumed.
  - `modalOverlay` consumes every change in Main, after its descendants.
  - So a result's press is cancelled by the first move dispatched between down and up.
- Failure scenario: New, type "lon", then tap "London" with a real finger. The finger drifts a pixel or two between down
  and up, well inside touch slop. No city is added and the search stays open. On 05e543d7 nothing consumed those moves,
  so the same tap picked the city. Device check, one run per build: `input swipe x y x+4 y 150` on a
  `clock_search_result:` row.
- The evidence cannot show this:
  - bd114d21 appears in exactly four logs, all of them L13_13, and L13_13 never taps a result.
  - E8, E10, E31 and EDGE_WORLD do tap results, but none was re-run on bd114d21.
  - Their `tap_node` is `input tap` (a down and an up, no move), so they would pass on either build.
- The commit says "its field and results take their own touches first". That holds for the down only.
- Also untested under the same root: scrolling the results (up to 50 rows of 44 dp), and a touch in the field (cursor,
  selection, bringing the keyboard back after Back hid it). Lens A should judge these; there is no evidence for them.
