# L13-14 and L13-15 fixes: review B (evidence, adversarial), 2026-09-28

Reviewed: 4a1b7841 (the fix) and 247d01f1 (the drivers, four runs and the ledger rows). I read the dumps, headers,
rings, driver and harness source, and the local APK's dex, extracted to scratch at 09:26. Read-only: no build and no

**Verdict: FAIL.** The rows stand. On the builds their headers name, each fails before the fix and passes after, and
the "row under the point" check is sound. One blocking finding: by code reading, the fix changes the expanded bar, and
no run covers that state.

## Blocking findings

   at 4a1b7841).
   - ClockScaffold's Box holds, bottom to top: the pager, then ClockAppBar's `OverlayLayer` with the full-size "…" scrim,
     then the bar (`ClockTabs.kt:104-105`, `ClockWidgets.kt:140` and `:166`). Hit testing stops at the first sibling it
     hits (r2-a, ui-android 1.11.4). The new bar target is always on.
   - Closed, the list lies under the bar and the target does its job. Open, the scrim lies under the bar, and the target
     takes its touches too. L13-14 (a)'s after-run measures exactly this cut-off.
   - Failure scenario: on any Clock tab, tap "…", then tap the bar's blank left part (x 60). On ce79bc17 the scrim takes
     it (`awaitFirstDown(); waitForUpOrCancellation() -> onExpand(false)`, `:144-145`) and the menu closes. On 9e658a94
     the bar takes it, consumes nothing, and the menu stays open.
   - This is outside the defect's scope and a regression in the bar. Neither driver opens "…". Code reading only.
     Mid-review I saw an uncommitted change that turns the target on only while the bar is collapsed; not reviewed.
