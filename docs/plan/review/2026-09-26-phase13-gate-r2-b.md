**N1 — E8's reminder-menu band is offset from what its `[motion]` line measures. Fix this before Jeremy's end-of-project run.**

- **The band.** E8 asserts settle = 249.7 ± 17 ms (e8.sh:67), so the floor is 232.7.
- **Lines below it on 509f6e49.** 5 of the 29 reminder_menu lines in saved rings read below that floor:
  - E8/ring-launcher.txt:408 reads 231. This is corroboration attempt 2; E8.txt:58 notes it, but nothing asserts it.
  - E9/ring-launcher.txt:196, 212, 219 and 254 read 230, 232, 232 and 230.
- **Lines below it on earlier builds.** E3/ring-launcher.txt:289 and E11_MENU/ring-launcher.txt:290 each read 232.
- **The cause is the clock, not the product.** Every one of these lines reads frames=17 and maxGapMs=17. `MotionTrace` measures settle from `t0 = SystemClock.uptimeMillis()`, taken when the `LaunchedEffect` starts (`RemindersPage.kt` `LongPressMenu`), to a vsync frame time. Where t0 falls inside the first frame shifts settle by up to one frame. The observed spread, 230–246, is one frame wide.
- **What the captures show.** The grow settles 250 ms after its half-height first frame (re-derived from rec-reminder_menu-3.mp4):
  - first frame at 1.4330 s;
  - that frame held once (region change 0.01 at 1.4492 s);
  - last change at 1.6834 s.

  That is exactly the doc's "233 ms + one frame".
- **What this means for the row.** The three asserted runs (E8.txt:25-33) are true passes, and the product meets the doc. But the band rejects about one open in six on a correct product. At the observed rate, a three-open run fails about 4 times in 10.
- **Fix.** Either stamp t0 from the half-height frame's vsync, or re-cut the band to cover the one-frame phase and assert every `[motion] reminder_menu` line in the row's slices, not three of them. Either way needs a Change Log line, because the doc is FINAL.
- **The pane.** It has the same t0 phase (241–258 in E8's ring), but that range sits inside its ± 17 band.
