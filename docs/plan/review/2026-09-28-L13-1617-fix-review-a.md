## L13-16 (checked, holds)

1. **Where the limit is measured.** Each row menu is composed in an OverlayLayer inside the tab's Box, the pager's page
   Box and ClockScaffold's content Box. All are fillMaxSize, and OverlayLayer and Box only loosen the minimums.
   - So `constraints.maxHeight` is the content Box's height: band bottom to nav bar top. The bar is aligned to its
     bottom, and the menu's y origin is the tab Box's top (`rootTop`).
   - The limit rounds 48 and 60 dp with the same `roundToPx` as the bar's and menu's `height` modifiers, so at grow = 1
     the menu's bottom meets the bar's top to the pixel, at any density.
2. **Every state a row menu can open in.**
   - Collapsed bar: 48 dp, so the limit is right. Expanded bar: the "…" scrim covers the list, so no hold starts, and
     L13-13's effect closes an open menu. Select: holds are off. Search: its page covers the list.
   - **Compare mode is also a menu state.** CityRow's detector does not check `compare`, and L13-13's effect fires
     only on a key change, so a hold there opens Remove. The strip's body is 48 dp, so the limit holds (note 2).
3. **placeRelative vs offset.** `offset(Dp, Dp)` is OffsetElement with rtlAware = true. OffsetNode measures with the
   incoming constraints, reports the placeable's size and calls `placeRelative(x.roundToPx(), y.roundToPx())`.
   - The new block is identical except for y, in LTR and RTL. The grow keeps its top edge because y uses the full
     `height`, as before. Hit bounds and tags follow placement.
4. **The finger under the menu.** Compose hit-tests a touch only at its down: `changedToDownIgnoreConsumed` gates
   `hitTest` and `addHitPath` in `process`. So the flyout, composed at the hold, is not in the held pointer's path.
   detectTapOrHold also consumes the release in Initial, so the lift reaches neither the verb nor the scrim.
5. **Scope.** The one change is at the producer (ClockFlyout's placement). The three row menus pass the inset.

## L13-17 (checked, holds)

1. **Disabled buttons: no change.** `clickable(enabled = false)` never calls onClick, so no collapse is written.
2. **Enabled buttons** (Add, Select, New, Compare, Pin, Share, select-mode Delete, the editors' Save and Delete) now
   collapse the bar, then act as before. Pin and Share start system UI over a collapsed bar.
3. **Nothing relied on the bar staying expanded.** Only ClockAppBar, the three row-menu effects and `back()` read
   `barExpanded`. No phase-15 driver taps a button on an expanded bar expecting it to stay (e10, l13_11, l13_13 and
   l13_14 use only "…", its items or Back).
4. **The write is plain,** like the menu items' `onExpand(false)`, so round 3's frame analysis of plain-write closes
   covers it. It sits at the producer: the shared ClockAppBar handles all three bars. (Its comment's "CommandBar" means
   W10M's platform control; no R7 or r11 row is cited for it.)

## Notes (none blocking)

1. **Unrecorded top clamp.** `coerceAtLeast(0)` also moves a menu asked for above the page top.
   - Before, it rose over the tab band; now it sits at the page top. This happens for a row whose top is more than 60 dp
     above the list's top: an alarm row showing under 28 dp, a city under 36.9 dp, a timer block under 115 dp (common).
   - Benign, since the verb stays in the page. But the KDoc, commit and ledger ("a menu with room below its row opens
     where it did") do not say it, and no driver runs it.
2. **Compare mode, not run on a device.** The menu is clamped to the strip's 48-dp body. The strip's 14 × 6 dp notch is
   drawn over the menu's 8-dp bottom padding at the centre. The notch takes no touches and the padding is no target, so
   the effect is cosmetic.
3. **The menu can open under the finger** (a finger within 60 dp above the bar).
   - R7 §3.6.2 puts the menu's bottom 23.5 epx above the touch point, so W10M never covers it. The old placement could
     too (a hold in an alarm row's bottom 28 dp).
   - The lift is harmless (item 4). What is left is a quick re-tap on the same spot, which lands on Delete or Remove
     with no confirm. Pre-existing in kind.
4. **Editor flyouts are unchanged, by code reading only.** With bottomInset 0, the limit is the page height minus the
   flyout height.
   - On the QA device the editor page is 704 dp. The days, sound and snooze flyouts end at 472, 506.7 and 527.1 dp, well
     inside their limits.
   - They would move only on a page under 527.1 dp: the activity is portrait-locked, and the IME does not shrink the page
     (`setDecorFitsSystemWindows(false)`, no IME padding).
   - Neither new driver opens one on 0c3e6ac7.
5. **L13-17 also fixes two symptoms the ledger does not list** (on 96dc4701, neither in l13_17_row.sh). The editors'
   Save or Delete on an expanded bar returned to the tabs with the "…" menu up. Select-mode Delete left the menu over
   the list.
6. **Round 3's note 1 is now reachable from a button too.** "…" toggles on the composed value, so a "…" tap in the frame
   after a button's collapse is swallowed. Pre-existing, harmless.
7. **Either shape elsewhere: none that leaks.**
   - The "…" menu is bar-anchored, the editor flyouts' fixed tops are clear of the bar, and the search list is padded.
   - Every path that leaves a mode clears the flag: `leaveTabModes` (tapTab, settleTab, show), Back, the menu items,
     and now the buttons.
   - Outside both shapes, pre-existing:
     - "…" → About from an editor, then Back, lands on the tabs (ClockNav.back's `else`) and drops unsaved edits.
     - An editor flyout stays open, visible, under the "…" scrim (the editors have no L13-13 effect).
