ADDENDUM, later on 2026-09-21: the two things above that "could not be captured" now ARE captured.

The AVD was never wedged and the session was never untappable. Two real faults were stacked:

  1. SystemUI's immersive-mode cling. The first time the shell hides the system bars, SystemUI puts
     a full-screen "Viewing full screen / To exit, swipe down from the top of your screen / Got it"
     window on top of everything. It is a SystemUI window, so a uiautomator dump shows only
     SystemUI and every tap lands on it -- including taps on Android's own dialogs. Cleared with
     `settings put secure immersive_mode_confirmations confirmed`, now in provision.sh.
  2. The session crashed before it drew. `ViewTreeLifecycleOwner not found from
     CortanaSession$onCreateContentView$host$1` at AbstractComposeView.onAttachedToWindow, twice out
     of two opens (22:05:04 and 22:06:00). The owners were set on the ComposeView, but Compose
     builds the window recomposer from the window's ROOT view, and the root is the instrumentation
     FrameLayout that wraps it. Fixed at the producer: the owners go on the view onCreateContentView
     returns. Zero crashes since.
