  44-epx pitch", G1 277 → 321).
- **Build:** `app-debug.apk` md5 `686506a7a7936b2e` (worktree commit 83754009).

"+ Email" (glyph top at y 1281 px) and "+ Address" (1467 px) follow one another with nothing but a group rule between
them: 186 px = 62.0 epx. The other pairs have a field between them ("+ Phone" → the e-mail field → "+ Email";
"+ Address" → Company → "+ Other"), so no two "+ field" rows anywhere in the editor are 44 epx apart.
What does hold: each row's node is 44 epx tall (PASS lines "the row is 44 epx tall"), and the rules are 1 epx of
(103,103,103) from x 12 to W − 12.

## What the spec says
E20: "… label cap top → box top 22.75, '+ field' rows at a 44-epx pitch, 1-epx (103,103,103) group rules (P4.1–P4.7)".
r11/people.md P4.6: "'+ Phone', '+ Email', '+ Ringtone', '+ Text alert tone', '+ Address', '+ Other': a 16-epx '+' and a
15-epx label at x 12; consecutive rows at 44-epx pitch" (MEDIUM, G1 277 → 321); P4.7: rules "about 22–23 epx clear
above and below" — in G1 the rules are at 113 / 254 / 359 / 420, so one group there held TWO "+" rows (277 and 321,
between the rules at 254 and 359) and the next group one.

## Diagnosis (one line)
`people/PeopleEditorPage.kt` `TypedGroup` starts every kind (phone, e-mail, address) with its own `GroupRule()` and
ends it with its one `AddRow`, so no group ever holds two "+ field" rows; the build's rule-to-rule distance for a
one-row group (62 epx) is r11's (61), but the 44-epx pitch between two rows of one group has nothing drawn to hold it.

## Also recorded, not graded (an r11 value the row does not word)
The "+" glyph's ink starts at x 14.0 epx on every row; P4.6 gives "a 16-epx '+' … at x 12" (MEDIUM).

## For the lead
Either the grouping is brought to W10M's (two "+" rows sharing a group, 44 epx apart), or the clause is re-cut by a
Change Log entry to what a one-row group can show ("each '+ field' row 44 epx tall; a one-row group 61–62 epx rule to
rule"). The driver asserts the clause as E20 words it and is not adapted.
