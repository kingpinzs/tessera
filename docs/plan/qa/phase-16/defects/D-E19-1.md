half-level crossings in epx"). `cal_geo.py` reads the same thing from this build's screencap: the left edge of the ink
(pixels at least half way from the background to the ink's core colour) of the text or glyph. The fixtures use R11's own
sample text where R11 names it, so the same leading glyph is compared.

## Diagnosis (one line)
The build places each text's or glyph's BOX at R11's number (`CalMetrics.EVENT_TITLE_X = 92.5f`, `PANE_ACCOUNT_X`, the
name at 62), while R11's number is where the INK starts; the glyph's own side bearing (the chevron glyph's 2.3 epx, "M" /
"m" 1.3 epx, "P" 1.7 epx at these sizes) is then added on top.

## For the lead / the owner to rule
- Read at the BOX, all three are exact (14.00, 62.00, 92.67) and the clauses would pass; read at the ink — R11's own
  method — they fail by 0.3 to 1.3 epx beyond the ± 1 tolerance.
- The title's case is the weakest of the three: R11 does not say which of C1 083's titles gave 92.5, so "Pay rent" is a
  likely, not a certain, like-for-like sample. The pane's two names and its chevron are like-for-like.
- H1 [fidelity] judges the same four screens on the phone.

## Not this defect (the same kept run)
