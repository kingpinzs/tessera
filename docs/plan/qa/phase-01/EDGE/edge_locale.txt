## DEFECT: the drawn clock did not follow a time zone change, and ignored the 12/24-hour setting
before the fix: with the device at 14:40 EDT the bar still read 12:40 PM (tz_clock_before.png / tz_clock_after.png)
cause: the process caches the default time zone, and the bar formatted with java.text.DateFormat (locale only, so the user's 24-hour setting had no effect)
fix: the bar drops the cached zone on the time zone / time / locale broadcasts, observes Settings.System.TIME_12_24, and formats with android.text.format.DateFormat
after the fix, with the shell running: cmd alarm set-timezone America/New_York -> 2:43 PM, back to America/Boise -> 12:43 PM (tz_live_*.png); time_12_24 24 -> "12:44", back to 12 -> "12:44 PM" (clock_12h.png, clock_24h.png, clock_12h_restored.png)
