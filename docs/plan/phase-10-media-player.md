# Phase 10 — W10M media player

## Goal

Jeremy, 2026-09-22: "Need a media player that feels and looks like the windows one."

A media player that belongs to the shell, in the W10M idiom the rest of this build is held to — so the
Music tile opens something that looks like it came off the phone rather than something Android happened
to resolve.

## Scope

**In:** to be settled by the interview. The fork that decides everything else is Q1 below.

**Out (explicitly):** cloud accounts, streaming services and online catalogues (A11: no cloud); anything
that needs a separately installed app (RV1).

## Interview queue (Stage A step 4)

- **Q1 — what the player IS.** With Jeremy. The answer decides the shape of every other question, so
  nothing else is asked until it lands.
- Q2 — where it lives: does it take over the MUSIC slot, or sit beside it as its own tile?
- Q3 — which W10M release to measure against (Groove Music's look changed across 8.1 → 10 → the final
  release), and whether R3/R6/R7 already carry any of it or a new measurement pass is needed.
- Q4 — the now-playing surface: the tile already grows and carries transport controls (INDEX item 3), so
  what does the full-screen player add beyond it.
- Q5 — the library: what it indexes, and what happens on a phone with no local audio at all.
- Q6 — the lock-screen / glance relationship (phase 07 territory).
