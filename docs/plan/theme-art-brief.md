# Theme pictures — what Jeremy generates (phase 12, interview Q6, 2026-09-23)

Jeremy: "(b) tell me what I need to do because I have both gpt and gemini personal accounts". The four
pictures below are generated by Jeremy on his PERSONAL ChatGPT or Gemini account (never a work tool), saved
into `art/themes/` in this repo, and the agent crops, scales and bundles them. The fifth preset with a picture,
"Windows 10 Mobile (original)", uses Microsoft's stock pictures instead (research item R12, branding module).

## Do this

1. In ChatGPT (image generation) or Gemini, paste one prompt below. Ask for PORTRAIT: in ChatGPT pick the tall
   / portrait size; in Gemini add "9:16 portrait". Make two or three of each and keep the ones you like.
2. Look before you save: no text, no logos, no faces, no watermark or sparkle mark in a corner. If an image has
   a visible mark, make it again or use the other account.
3. Save as PNG into `art/themes/` with these names (a, b, c for variants):
   `hal-a.png`, `soft-a.png`, `lumia-a.png`, `midnight-a.png` (then `-b`, `-c`).
4. Tell me when they are there. I pick the crop for the S25 Ultra (1440 × 3120, with Start's 1.3× parallax
   headroom), scale them, bundle them, and you judge the result on the phone (a NEEDS-HUMAN row).

Why these constraints: Start's tiles are see-through, so the picture shows behind them — busy detail or bright
spots under the tiles make the labels hard to read. Keep the middle calm.
